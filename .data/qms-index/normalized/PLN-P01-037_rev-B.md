SCOPE
This plan applies to all components and materials purchased for the MX1 Portable X-Ray System.
This plan includes the following supplier qualification requirements:
Remote review of supplier quality systems (without onside supplier facility audit). This requirement may be satisfied by completion of QSF-022 - Supplier Questionnaire.
Certification to a recognized third party or accreditation.
First Article Inspection (FAI), except for off-the-shelf components.
Supplier Quality Agreements for suppliers of services or custom components.
Supplier facility audit of their quality systems and manufacturing/inspection capabilities.  Refer to section 7 of this document.
The above supplier assessment and qualification activities shall be performed as required per criteria provided in this plan and summarized in Appendix 1.
BACKGROUND
A supplier quality plan is a required Phase 3 deliverable of the design control process per QSP-018 - Design Control. The Supplier Quality Plan will be updated to document the closure of outstanding supplier qualification activities and released in Design Phase 4 per DHF-008.
Supplier and/or MedAI inspection to gauge repeatability and reproducibility (R&R) qualification may be performed, per a separate protocol, if capability and/or inspection agreement requirements are not met. This will be documented in a report related to such qualification.
It is planned to complete all required supplier qualification activities before the product is released for commercial distribution. It may be necessary and appropriate for some supplier qualification activities to extend beyond the product release. When activities extend beyond product release, it will be detailed in the report, including justification and a plan for completion.
Supplier qualification and monitoring activities will be completed both by MedAI and Sanmina, the selected Contract Manufacturing Organization (CMO) for the MX1 Portable X-Ray System. Per the MedAI-Sanmina Quality Plan, 555-0005-Q Quality Plan for MedAI Inc:
MedAI shall either provide Sanmina with a list of approved suppliers per component or designate the supplier as “Open Source” for a specific component.
MedAI-approved suppliers shall be qualified and approved by MedAI. Sanmina will select and qualify Open Source suppliers.
MedAI will provide final approval for all Open Source suppliers.
Sanmina shall be responsible for the ongoing management of all suppliers.
MedAI will also perform supplier monitoring for all approved suppliers in the QSR-004 Approved Suppliers Log every two years as required by QSP-007 - Supplier Management.

DEFINITIONS
Supplier
The direct provider of the product, component or service that directly affects the quality of a finished device or accessory distributed by MedAI, or affects the quality of a system established at MedAI.
Product Supplier
Suppliers providing components, manufacturing materials, in-process devices, finished devices, returned devices or equipment that may affect the quality of a finished device or accessory distributed by MedAI.
Component
Any raw material, substance, piece, part, software, labeling, or assembly which is intended to be included as part of the finished, packaged, and labeled device. This includes any off-the-shelf components or custom parts intended to be included as part of the finished device, packaging and/or labeling.
Service Suppliers (Contractors and Consultants)
Suppliers that provide contracting or consulting services for parts of the manufacturing or quality system (i.e. contracted warehousing, manufacturing, documentation/labeling translations, equipment calibrations, etc.).
SUPPLIER QUALIFICATION ACTIVITIES
Supplier Tiers
Suppliers are separated into three tiers based on risk. Tier 1 contains the highest-risk (critical) suppliers and Tier 3 contains low-risk suppliers. Per QSP-007, the following evaluation criteria are defined for each tier:
Supplier Quality Systems/Survey Review
All supplier quality systems shall be evaluated. Supplier quality systems may be assessed through completion of QSF-022 - Supplier Questionnaire or equivalent.
All suppliers will have a “Yes” in the QMS Review/Survey column of Appendix 1 in this plan.
MedAI Quality personnel shall review the quality systems of the MX1 component suppliers marked with a “Yes” in Appendix 1. This level of review does not include a facility audit. This review shall include:
The completion of of the Supplier Assessment Form, QSF-027, and
Obtaining copies of the supplier’s current certificates as verification.
The Supplier Assessment Form and certificates shall be electronically stored within the supplier file (located in “Vendors” drive).
First Article Inspection (FAI)
FAIs shall be performed for all parts and/or services that are unique to the product line and custom designed to MedAI specifications. Suppliers meeting these criteria will be identified with a “Yes” in the FAI column of Appendix 1. Otherwise a “No” will be entered in the FAI column of Appendix 1.
FAIs shall be performed on the first lot of each component at the current revision but only one FAI per vendor is required for vendor approval. FAI requirements for each part shall be determined by engineering. In some cases, it may be determined that capability over multiple lots be assessed. In this case, Cpk or Ppk targets will be determined by engineering. Documentation of any FAI requirements beyond normal receiving inspection and results of the FAI shall be filed with the receiving documentation for each part.
Supplier Quality Systems/Facility Audit
A facility audit shall be performed for all Tier 1 suppliers. Additionally, if QA determines that based on risk, a supplier audit is warranted, a quality system and/or facility audit may be performed for Tier 2 and Tier 3 suppliers. Generally, if an audit is determined to be necessary, the supplier/part compared to other suppliers/parts will have relatively greater product performance risk or MedAI manufacturing cost/business risk (component variation/nonconformance or unreliable component supply).
Suppliers meeting these criteria will be identified with a “Yes” in the Audit column of Appendix 1. Otherwise a “No” will be entered in the Audit column of Appendix 1.
An audit plan will be written by the MedAI auditor and sent to the supplier at least 2 weeks before the audit date. A desktop audit may be performed in place of an on-site audit for quality system audits.
Following completion of any supplier audits, the auditor shall generate an audit report and provide the supplier with a copy. The audit report shall be filed within the supplier file.
Supplier Quality Agreement
All suppliers that provide a service or custom components designed to MedAI specifications shall sign QSF-083 - Supplier Quality Agreement, or an equivalent agreement, prior to final supplier approval. Suppliers meeting these criteria will be identified with a “Yes” in the Quality Agreement column of Appendix 1. Otherwise, “N/A” will be entered in the Quality Agreement column of Appendix 1.
QA personnel shall obtain a signed copy of the Quality Agreement from the supplier. Once the supplier has signed the agreement, MedAI QA personnel will countersign the agreement and send a copy to the supplier. The fully executed agreement shall be electronically stored within the supplier file.
Final Approval
Suppliers may be “Conditionally Approved” if all qualification activities other than a quality system/facility audit or FAI are complete and satisfactory. The Supplier Assessment Form shall state all outstanding items required for final approval. Conditionally Approved status will be noted in Appendix 1.
All outstanding items for Conditionally Approved suppliers shall be completed in Phase 4. If activities extend beyond product release, it will be detailed in this plan, including justification and a plan for completion
Following the completion of the conditional approval action items, the Supplier Assessment Form shall be updated with final approval status.
INITIAL INVENTORY TRANSFER
MedAI has provided Sanmina with components that have an Open Source designation in the MedAI Approved Vendor List per BOM-055 for use in approximately 50 units during Process Qualification and the initial manufacturing period of MX1 following Phase 4 closure. Because these components were supplied by MedAI-selected vendors and will be used one time for a limited number of builds, the suppliers of those components will not be qualified and approved per MedAI’ Supplier Management procedure, QSP-007. All components have been inspected and qualified, and all MX1 units will undergo 100% in-process verification testing; therefore, supplier-related risk for the affected components is minimal. Note: MedAI supplied components to Sanmina from Rush PCB, a supplier who was not able to reliably supply conforming product. MedAI performed 100% inspection on all Rush components. Rush PCB has been inactivated as a supplier within the MedAI QMS; Sanmina will not place any future orders with Rush PCB following use of the MedAI-supplied inventory.
Additionally, components from Protolabs have been approved for use in up to 500 production units per the Conditional Approval requirements defined in the Protolabs supplier assessment forms. Protolabs has been identified as unable to reliably provide dimensionally conforming product; however, Protolabs parts are able to be verified via physical inspections and CoCs denoting adherence to material specifications.  MedAI has invested in a number of injection molds from Protolabs and the executive team has made a financial decision to use Protolabs as an initial supplier for early production launch. In order to mitigate any supplier-related risk, MedAI will be ordering all Protolabs-supplied parts and consigning them to Sanmina and will require minimum AQL 1.0 inspection of all components supplied by Protolabs with no option for “dock-to-stock” acceptance. Additionally, MedAI will only order existing injection molded components from Protolabs, and not any new molded parts or machined parts. A new supplier will be selected, qualified, and approved following the manufacturing of up to 500 MX1 units, and Protolabs will be made inactive on the MedAI Approved Vendor List in QSR-004.
SERVICING
Prior to completion of servicing activities, MedAI shall ensure that all inventory used during servicing is provided by approved suppliers.
OUTSTANDING ITEMS FOR CONDITIONALLY APPROVED SUPPLIERS
At time of Phase 4 closure, Sanminna Carrollton shall remain conditionally approved as the supplier of the MX1 PCBAs. Conditional approval will extend into Phase 5 as multiple PCBAs failed first article inspection. The corrective and preventive actions are being implemented by Sanmina and documented per QSP-009 Nonconforming Material Control. The remaining quantity of PCBAs ordered under the same POs with failing FAIs will be inspected per their AQL, dispositioned, and reworked as necessary by MedAI before Sanmina uses them in production. The first article inspection will be repeated on the next order of PCBAs. Sanmina may be approved as the supplier of the MX1 PCBAs upon passing first article inspection results.Note: Sanmina Carrollton was approved as the Contracting Manufacturing Organization for MX1 following passing FAI results of an MX1 PQ unit. See PO22655 Incoming Inspection Record.
