Purpose
The MX1 architecture diagram is a high level block diagram for the MX1 Portable X-Ray System and defines all the major device components and their associated subassemblies. The list of major device components is as follows:
E1: Emitter
C1: Cassette
F1: Wireless Foot Pedal
Each device component is broken out into its major systems. Specifically, the major mechanical systems, PCBAs, and power components. Interconnections are drawn and labeled between the components to define on a high level the wire harnesses and the port layout.
