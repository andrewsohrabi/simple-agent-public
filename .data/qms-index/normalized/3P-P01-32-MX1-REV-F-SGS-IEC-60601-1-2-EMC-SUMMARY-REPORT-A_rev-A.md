Purpose
The purpose of this assessment was to evaluate the electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) of the MX1 Portable X-ray System Rev F which incorporates modified PCBAs into the E1 Emitter, C1 Cassette and W1 Wireless Charger, and an alternative NVMe module. Although EMC performance was not expected to change due to these device improvements, testing was performed to demonstrate MX1 Rev F maintains CISPR 11 Class A compliance.
Scope and Background
This assessment applies to the MX1 Portable X-Ray System Rev F, which consists of the E1 Emitter, C1 Cassette, H1 Wired Charger, and Collimation Pucks as well as the MX1 optional accessories F1 Foot Pedal and K1 Cart. The T1 Tablet was provided as support equipment.
Based on results presented in 3P-P01-26 Rev A, the worst case radiated emissions test configuration, MX1 with W1 and C1 plugged into H1 Wired Chargers, was evaluated for radiated emissions.
Conducted emissions were evaluated with K1, C1 and W1 plugged into the test equipment.
This assessment was performed in accordance with IEC 60601-1-2 Ed. 4.1: General requirements for basic safety and essential performance – Collateral Standard: Electromagnetic disturbances – Requirements and tests.
Per the MX1 Instructions for Use (IFU-MX1 Rev. G), It is intended for use by qualified/trained medical professionals on adult, infant, and neonate patients for orthopedic radiographic, orthopedic serial radiographic, orthopedic fluoroscopic, and orthopedic interventional procedures of only shoulders to fingers and knees to toes. The device is to be used in healthcare facilities where qualified operators are present.
EMC failure may result in loss of device functionality or interference with other devices. This may present risks to patients and operators in any case of delayed procedure or interference with other medical devices.

Procedure and Results Summary
Equipment and Materials
The following MX1 Portable X-Ray System Rev F components, K1 Cart Rev D, and supporting equipment were provided to SGS to be used for the assessment.
Table 1: Unit Under Test Information
Spare E1(PN MS-10010) and C1 (PN MS-10083) Battery Packs
The assessment was conducted by SGS North America Inc at 620 Oak Road NW, Lakeville, IL 60002 using the procedures described in Attachment 1.
Device configuration for all testing was the final finished version. No modifications were required.  Note, W1, MS-10514, was built to BOM Rev D-alpha.
The device was built with all WorkStations with the exception of the End of Line WorkStation (WS-012) as camera and focal spot calibration, time of flight and detector calibration, laser alignment, and burn-in were not required.
The MX1 System was configured with software v4.0.0-alpha.
Based on the SGS EMC Test Report in Attachment 2, the MX1 Portable X-ray System, Rev F, complies with the requirements indicated in IEC 60601-1-2 Ed. 4.1 and testing was conducted per the SGS test plan in Attachment 1.
EMC testing demonstrated the MX1 Portable X-ray System, Rev F, complies with CISPR 11 Class A limits for equipment intended for use in industrial, commercial or business environments.
The MX1 device is considered to be a Group 1 device which generates RF energy necessary for its internal functioning.
MX1 System Configurations assessed during Conducted Emissions conform to the test plan.  Each individual component (W1 charging E1, C1, and cart (K1) was evaluated representing wireless charging configurations with and without the cart and capturing the worst case intended configuration.
