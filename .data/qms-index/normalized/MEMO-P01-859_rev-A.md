Appendix 1 - Post Phase 4 Closure Meeting Update
Upon holding the Phase 4 Closure meeting on Thursday, 20Mar2025, MedAI understood Sanmina, the MX1 contract manufacturer, to have verified that the 66% passing criteria of their MX1 PQ had been met with the defined rework levels. However, on Friday, 21Mar2025, Sanmina determined that the rework levels demonstrated that the process needed improvement to hit their demand and yield targets. This does not affect the quality or acceptance of units produced given they passed each qualified manufacturing test and inspection, which represents 100% verification of each unit. Therefore, Sanmina released the units to MedAI. This agreement is reflected in the Premarket Approval Agreement, signed 21Mar 2025
The closure of this Phase 4 represents approval to release MX1 units to customers with the knowledge that, while the contract manufacturer (Sanmina) PQ is incomplete due to the need to improve yield, each unit shipped will have completed 100% verification to requirements by passing each qualified manufacturing test and inspection.  All manufacturing equipment has completed qualification testing while establishing the manufacturing process and initial part qualification supplied by approved vendors inspected consistent with incoming inspection procedures. The product design has been fully verified, including safety testing.
