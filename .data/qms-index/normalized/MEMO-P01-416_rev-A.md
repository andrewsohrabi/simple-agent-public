1. Purpose
Prior to the start of the P01 project and as part of the due diligence for fundraising an IP Review was completed. This IP Review was performed by the law firm of Smith Davis Jones LLP and completed in 2020.
As part of this review a Freedom to Operate (FTO) study was conducted to ensure that the MedAI Imager P00 and the future P01 product had the ability to develop and make a product without legal liabilities.
3. Discussion
Based on the excerpt from the email correspondence with Jordan Davenport (provided in Reference section) and the more detailed report (not provided in this document) MedAI has concluded that we have little to no risk of the Imager P00 and P01 infringing on any third party patent rights and have sufficient Freedom to Operate in this space.
