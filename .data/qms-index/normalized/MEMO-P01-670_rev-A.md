STUDY PURPOSE
This document describes Formative Usability Evaluation of the Emitter Fan Cleaning procedure for the MX1 Portable X-Ray System. This study is intended to gather feedback from MedAI employees not involved in the design or maintenance of the Emitter’s front fan housing in order to inform the design process.
OBJECTIVE
This Formative Usability effort aims to evaluate the design and instructions for the MX1 Emitter fan cleaning steps, as defined in the MX1 Instructions for Use. The collected feedback will be used to evaluate the cleaning process and any necessary changes, and will be considered for and incorporated into future execution of the design.

METHODS
Test Environment
This study was conducted at MedAI Headquarters in Springfield, Illinois.
Test Participants
Test participants consisted of MedAI personnel who have not had prior experience designing, modifying, or disassembling the MX1 Emitter, in particular, the front fan housing.
Test Personnel
Test personnel consisted of MedAI personnel who have prior knowledge of MX1 System design and protocols.
Procedure
The steps were as follows, intending to cover the typical fan cleaning process and facilitate feedback:
First allow the test participant to get familiar with the instruction set that they will be using during the protocol
The provided instruction set will be the same as they would be when presented to true users in the IFU included with the MX1 System. See Appendix A - Fan Cleaning Instructions for the instruction set.
Test personnel will give the following introduction:
We will have you complete the Emitter fan cleaning procedure as listed in the IFU. This is the same procedure users will follow once a year. We’ve given you all the necessary supplies to complete the maintenance. While we will not be able to answer any questions, we do want to know what parts of the instructions or the procedure itself are unclear.
Please pretend the Emitter is turned on for the first step of the procedure. When you reach the compressed air step, we have provided a can of antifreeze that has the same toggling mechanism. However, please only spray a small amount and simulate the rest of the cleaning.
The test participant will then be allowed to complete the cleaning procedure at their own pace.
Test personnel will log any questions or feedback relayed by the test participant, as well as any difficulty or confusion exhibited while completing the procedure.
If, during the screw plug removal, test personnel notice the test participant having difficulty with removal by hand, they are to provide the flathead screwdriver.
Once the test participant completes the procedure, test personnel will ask the following questions to facilitate any additional feedback:
Do you think that process was difficult in any way?
What was the hardest step?
Were the instructions either confusing or unclear at any point?
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
There were no defined acceptance criteria for this evaluation; rather, this study serves as an assessment of the MX1 Emitter Fan Cleaning Protocol to identify undue complexity within the instructions or process. The data collected will be evaluated to inform further design iterations.

General notes:
It is noted that the screw plugs will be damaged from routine cleaning over time.
The Fan Duct ASSY had to be replaced a total of three times (with additional scrap assemblies) due to wear caused by disassembly and reassembly. One of these times was from user error, but the other two times occurred naturally over the course of multiple users completing the procedure.
A few images showing the stripped material observed during inspection were recorded. These can be viewed in Appendix B - Images Captured During the Study.
Next Steps
Over the course of this study, various usability issues were able to be identified for improvement. A majority of these issues involved either the wording or the indicators included in the instruction set. A list of suggestions, including adding clearer images and more detail, were recorded and will be implemented in a future revision.
Remaining issues with the design are currently being discussed, and suggested design changes are being considered for a future revision of the design. With the design as is, the fan cleaning procedure poses a risk of damage to the device. Therefore, the Emitter fan cleaning procedure will be removed from the MX1 Instructions for Use in favor of instead including a recommendation that users should send the device to MedAI for yearly cleaning and maintenance.
The fan cleaning procedure instruction set will then become a servicing instruction to be given to trained MedAI personnel in order to complete the cleaning. This is a potentially temporary change, and, given the appropriate design changes are implemented (such as those suggested above), this procedure may return to being executable by the user.

Appendix A: MX1 Fan Cleaning Instructions
Emitter Dust Removal
Shut down the Emitter before cleaning and disconnect any cords, connections, or other accessories.
To remove the Emitter’s fan cover, first remove the four screw covers and then, using a #1 Phillips Head screwdriver, unscrew all four screws; set aside all components.
Clean around the fan and between the fan blades using a can of compressed air or light blower.
Once all visible dust has been removed, replace the fan cover and all four screws using the #1 Phillips screwdriver, ensuring that the screws are appropriately tightened.
Replace all four screw covers by pressing them flush with the enclosure.
Power the Emitter and confirm the fan begins to function.
