1. Purpose
This memo documents the engineering feasibility work completed for the MX1, performed during development.
2. Scope
The information contained in this document reflects the MX1 design and includes testing and assessments performed on this design to confirm design direction and begin efforts to establish W1 performance.
3. Discussion
4.1  EMC Emissions and Immunity Testing. See Attachment 1
This study was to assess the EMC profile, per 60601-1-2 of the MX1 system and to implement improvements.This system was found to be compliant to Class B Radiated Emissions requirements; however, the device is only required to be compliant with Class A Radiated Emissions requirements per ANSI C53.4.
4.2. ESD Conductive Testing. See MEMO-P01-468 - P01 ESD Conductive Points Test_A.
This study was to determine potential conduction points in the MX1 system to verify design and investigate weak points. The tests demonstrated that ESD is not expected to present any issues and that all concerns are mitigated.
4.3. Emitter Drop and Impact Testing. See MEMO-P01-482 - 11/14/22 Emitter Drop and Impact Tests _A.
This study was to test the MX1 system susceptibility to drop and impact tests and to understand weak points and areas of improvements.
4.4 Tracking LED Setting Investigation. See MEMO-P01-664 - Tracking LED Setting Investigation_A.
This study was to understand the interference between the ToF and Tracking system and what mitigation measures are needed in order to improve the results. Some cracking and damage was observed; the memo lists improvements that have now been implemented.
4.5 Thermal Testing
Thermal testing for the Cassette and Emitter are underway now and results will be amended once tests are complete.
