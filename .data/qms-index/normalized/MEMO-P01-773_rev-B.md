SUMMARY OF ACTION ITEMS
DISCUSSION
Moxtek Tube Additional Verification
Additional Moxtek tube testing was planned prior to Phase 3 closure due to a lack of formal traceability for the Moxtek tubes used for initial verification and validation testing. However, all verification and validation testing performed on the initial Moxtek tubes passed. Due to a delay in receiving traceable Moxtek tubes installed in Monoblocks additional verification testing for due diligence will be performed during Phase 4. At this time, traceable Moxtek tubes have not been released to customers. Commercialization is gated by Phase 4 completion.
Battery Accuracy Testing
After further discussion, it was determined to assess battery accuracy the battery model should be verified to ensure the accuracy of the Open Circuit Voltage to State of Charge function and the coulomb counter of the Fuel Gage on both the Emitter and Cassette Battery Packs, MS-10010 and MS-10083, respectively. Testing performed in VVPR-P01-235 demonstrated the battery packs passed acceptance criteria for all fuel gauge monitoring accuracy tests including: voltage monitoring accuracy, current monitoring accuracy, fuel gauge coulomb count accuracy and fuel gauge voltage model accuracy. As these are all of the major parameters used by the fuel gauge to monitor the State of Charge of the battery packs, MS-10010 and MS-10083 have been demonstrated to report intuitive State of Charge forecasts to the MX1 Software System.
DICOM Justification
The analysis performed in MEMO-P01-660 demonstrates we are setting the pixels to the same values as previously tested so any variation would be due to the hardware (tablet display). We may consider performing additional DICOM testing during Phase 4 for due diligence.
Miscellaneous
A new Risk Management Report (RSK-P01-017) was generated instead of revising the existing report (RSK-P01-014).
