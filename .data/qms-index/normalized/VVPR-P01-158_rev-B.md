Protocol Section
STUDY PURPOSE
The purpose of this test protocol is to verify the MX1 Portable X-ray System meets foot pedal operating distance specifications in DR-P01-005 - Design Inputs & Specifications.
OBJECTIVE
To ensure that foot pedal may be used as intended within the specified range.
REFERENCESa
DR-P01-005 Rev. A - MX1 Design Inputs
MATERIALS
Materials and Equipment
MX1 Portable X-ray System:
MX1 Cassette (C1)
MX1 Emitter (E1)
MX1 Foot pedal (F1)
Tablet (T1) with MedAI Device App (APP)
PPE as necessary (gloves, eye protection, lead vest, etc)
T-063 - MX1 Positioning Jig
EQP-033 - Tape Measure
SAMPLE SIZE
Based on sample size constraints due to MX1 being capital equipment, and the worst case nature of this test, an n=1 will be used.
For this test, one (1) MX1 Portable X-ray System will be used.
METHODS
Locations and Personnel Responsibilities
This study shall take place in MedAI facilities.
The Quality Department (QA) is responsible for assigning individuals for the execution of the protocol.
Note - When possible, in order to maintain internal independence of review, MedAI shall assign internal staff members that are not involved in a particular design or its implementation, but who have sufficient knowledge to evaluate the project, to conduct the verification and validation activities.
Training Requirements - This study does not require any special training.
Experimental Procedure
The following requirements require emitting radiation. Wear radiation safety PPE (lead vest) and radiation monitor badge while performing the following steps.
Place the emitter and cassette in the T-063 - MX1 Positioning Jig. Position the T-063 jig such that the tracking system will allow an x-ray exposure in any mode.
Pair the Foot Pedal (F1) to the MX1 System, if not paired already.
Using EQP-033 Tapered Thickness Gauge (Tape Measure), measure and mark a 12 foot or greater distance from the MX1 System.
Place the foot pedal at the mark and perform the following:
Cycle through all modes (Fluoroscopy, Single Radiography, DDR, Photography), confirming on the Emitter
Trigger one single-exposure x-ray with the foot pedal; Confirm image appears on the MedAI Device App
Trigger one DDR exposure between 2 and 9 seconds long; Confirm image appears on the MedAI Device App
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
The foot pedal shall be able to trigger static and dynamic x-rays and change modes at a distance of 12 feet or more from the emitter. The MX1 System must pass all specifications listed in Table 1 with zero failures. No variable data analysis is required.
If it becomes necessary to make changes to the test procedures or study scope after testing has been initiated the protocol changes and associated rationale will be documented in the test report as deviations to the protocol.
