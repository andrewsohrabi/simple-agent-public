Purpose
This document describes the Use Specification for the MX1 Portable X-Ray System, concerning the intended use, including users, use cases, and use environment.
This Use Specification considers the requirements as defined in IEC 62366-1: 1.0 2015-02, as well as a preliminary analysis of user tasks in regards to the use of the MX1 Portable X-ray System.

Use Specification
Medical Indication
The MX1 Portable X-ray System is designed to aid clinicians with point-of-care visualization and guidance during X-rays of extremities and shoulders. It is intended for use in clinical environments and is not intended for surgical applications.
The MX1 Portable X-ray System is indicated for use by qualified/trained medical professionals on adult patients for orthopedic radiographic, orthopedic serial radiographic, orthopedic fluoroscopic, and orthopedic interventional procedures of only shoulders to fingers and knees to toes.  The device is not intended for use during surgery.  The device is not intended to replace a stationary radiographic system.The device is to be used in healthcare facilities where qualified operators are present (e.g., outpatient clinics, urgent cares, imaging centers, sports medicine facilities, occupational medicine clinics)
The MX1 System is contraindicated as follows:
The MX1 System is NOT intended for bariatric patients.
The MX1 System is NOT intended for mammography.
The MX1 System is NOT intended for dental applications.
The MX1 System is NOT intended to come in contact with non-intact skin.
The MX1 System is NOT intended for cardiac applications.The MX1 System is NOT intended for use in proximity to pacemakers or implantable cardioverter-defibrillators (ICDs)
The device is not intended to be used in environments with the following characteristics:
Direct sunlight, not including windowed sunlight
Home or residential settings or other settings where qualified operators are not present
Vehicular and moving environments, such as a moving ambulance
Aseptic or sterile fields, such as an open surgical field
Oxygen-rich environments, such as neonate incubators with supplemental oxygen
Strong magnetic fields, such as near an operating MRI machine
Patient Population
MX1 is indicated for use on adult patients that require diagnostic imaging of anatomy. This does not include bariatric patients for the indicated anatomies, namely body extremities.
Applied Anatomy
MX1 is indicated only for extremities, specifically shoulders to fingers and knees to toes, as written in the indications for use.
The Cassette, specifically its Active Area, is an applied part that is placed under the patient anatomy during an imaging procedure, with or without draping. Otherwise, a patient has transient or no contact with the rest of the system. During normal use, the Emitter is held above the patient anatomy during an imaging procedure and does not come into contact with the patient. An operator may operate the MX1 System with or without gloves.
User Profile
Indicated User Populations
Qualified medical personnel having received X-ray imaging training, such as physicians and radiation technologists, who have been further trained in the use of the MX1 System and have read the Instructions for Use and accompanying documentation.
Role-based User Groups: Two role-based user groups have been identified from customer interviews and historical association with two major ‘functions’: Radiography and Fluoroscopy. It can be anticipated that MX1 use with these two groups may exhibit different steps, user expectations, and use errors:
Healthcare workers trained to acquire radiographic imaging studies in their local jurisdictions (Group 1): These users may classically operate radiographic equipment to acquire ordered imaging studies then send the studies for review or review if qualified. These users generally do not perform fluoroscopic guidance without special training. Example job titles include but are not limited to:
Radiological Technologists (Rad Techs)
Nurse Practitioners, including DNP or MSN
Advanced Practice Providers (Physician's Assistants)
Physicians (MD or others) and Advanced Practice Providers that may conduct Fluoroscopy (Group 2): While this user group often orders and reads imaging studies as part of their clinical evaluation, they may operate x-ray equipment for fluoroscopic guidance as part of procedure work (e.g. surgical instrument guidance). Example job titles include but are not limited to:
Emergency Medicine Doctor
Orthopedist or Orthopedic Surgeon
Intensivists
Hospitalist
Internist
Interventional Radiologist
Podiatrist
Family Practice Physicians

Training
Upon setup of a new facility, MedAI personnel shall train and document at least one designated trainer in each facility responsible for training current and future employees of that facility on safe usage of the device. This Trainer will be educated and equipped with the resources in order to train new users at their facility.  Responsible organizations are responsible for ensuring designated Trainers are available to train any new users and any new users are trained according to a safe use training guide supplied by MedAI.
All users must be trained on the safe operation of the MX1 system before they use the device, per the instructions in the Instructions for Use. MedAI Personnel or a facility Trainer are responsible for training new users. The training gives an overview of using the MX1 for an radiological exam, information particular to MX1 or MedAI products, and safety considerations for use.
Operating Principle
The MX1 system accomplishes its uses through two major system components, the Emitter and Cassette, as well as additional accessories that add additional functionality.
The Emitter generates configurable X-ray beams which, in combination with collimation and additional control features, transmit radiation to the Active Area of the Cassette when fired.
The Cassette collects X-ray energy and provides a digital representation for display using a flat panel detector.
The MX1 tracking system utilizes IR LEDs and a camera to provide position and orientation data to be used in safety system interlocks, X-ray field estimation for puck and automated collimation and generating viewfinder overlays shown on the Emitter display.
The MedAI MX1 App provides an interface to view and manage images captured by the MX1 System.
The Emitter and Cassette can be transported in and between environments by their handles.
The wired charger provides power to charge the Cassette and Emitter
The wireless Foot Pedal allows the user to change between X-ray modes and trigger emissions via wireless connection to the Emitter.
The Collimation Pucks can be attached to the Emitter to collimate the X-ray beam to very small field sizes beyond the capabilities of the automatic collimation system.
