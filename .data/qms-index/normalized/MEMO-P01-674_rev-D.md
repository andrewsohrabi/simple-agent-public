PURPOSE
The purpose of this document is to assess whether the MX1 Portable X-Ray System, F1 Wireless Foot Pedal, and the K1 Cart are safe for use in their intended use environments with respect to biocompatibility.

DEFINITIONS
Biocompatibility - ability of a medical device or material to perform with an appropriate host response in a specific application
Biological Risk - combination of the probability of harm to health occurring as a result of adverse reactions associated with medical device or material interactions, and the severity of that harm
Biological Safety - freedom from unacceptable biological risk in the context of the intended use
Contact:
Direct Contact - medical device or medical device component that comes into physical contact with body tissue
Indirect contact - term used for a device or device component through which a fluid or gas passes, prior to the fluid or gas coming into physical contact with body tissue (in this case the device or device component itself does not physically contact body tissue)
Non-contact - term used for a device or device component that has no direct or indirect contact with the body (e.g., stand-alone software or database), and for which no biocompatibility information would be needed other than confirmation that there is no contact with the human body
Transient contact - term used for a device or device component that comes into very brief/transient contact with body tissue (e.g., hypodermic needles that are used for less than one minute)
Material - synthetic or natural polymer, metal or alloy, ceramic, or composite, including tissue rendered nonviable, used as a medical device or any part thereof
Toxic - capable of causing an adverse biological response
BACKGROUND
MX1: MX1 X-ray System is a portable and handheld device consisting of an Emitter, Cassette, Collimation Pucks, and a Wired Charger (qty 2). During normal use, the operator may operate the device either with or without gloves. If used without gloves, the operator will directly contact the device for a limited (< 24 hours) duration. The MX1 cassette may be patient contacting if bags and/or drapes are not used. All other components are non-patient contacting and any incidental contact that was to occur with the patient would be transient.
F1: The Wireless Foot Pedal is optional accessory provided with the MX1 system so that the operator can remotely trigger x-ray acquisition. Limited direct contact is expected of the operator (picking up to place foot pedal on the floor, move, etc); the foot pedal is non-patient contacting.
H1: The Wired Charger may be connected to both the Emitter and Cassette for charging. Limited direct contact is expected of the operator (handling charger to plug into emitter or cassette); the charger is non-patient contacting.
K1: The Cart is an optional accessory to MX1 that stores, transports, charges, and positions the MX1 system during use. Limited direct contact is expected of the operator (adjusting the device supports, transport, plugging and unplugging the cord); the Cart is non-patient contacting.
BIOCOMPATIBILITY EVALUATION
Each of the MX1 components requiring biocompatibility assessment (those which have the potential for intact skin contact) are included in Table 1 below. Those components include the Emitter and Cassette enclosures, buttons, displays, and keypads as well as the Collimation Pucks, labels, Wired Charger, and Foot Pedal cover/base. Repeated limited use with the operator was considered in the MX1 risk assessment, RSK-P01-010, and any potential biological effects are not considered to have a cumulative effect.
Per Guidance for Industry and Food and Drug Administration Staff - Use of International Standard ISO 10993-1, "Biological evaluation of medical devices - Part 1: Evaluation and testing within a risk management process" (2023), the recommended endpoints for surface device contact with intact skin for a limited (<24 hour) duration are cytotoxicity, sensitization, and irritation/intracutaneous reactivity. There are no additional FDA recommended endpoints for consideration.
Many of the MX1 materials are included in Attachment G of Guidance for Industry and Food and Drug Administration Staff - Use of International Standard ISO 10993-1, "Biological evaluation of medical devices - Part 1: Evaluation and testing within a risk management process" and have a long history of safe use. This guidance is referenced in Table 1 below as applicable. For the  components with materials not included in the Guidance for Industry and Food and Drug Administration Staff, a more detailed literature search was performed with references listed as applicable in Table 1 and are included for reference in the Appendices.
Table 1: Biocompatibility Assessment Summary
DISCUSSION
The MX1 Portable X-Ray System, along with the F1 Foot Pedal and K1 Cart, are not likely to cause cytotoxicity, sensitization, irritation or intracutaneous reactivity. It is unlikely that any adverse reactions would occur when in contact with intact skin, making the system safe for its intended use.
