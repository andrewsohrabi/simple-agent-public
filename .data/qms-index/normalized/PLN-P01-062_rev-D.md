Purpose
This document is intended to provide the project quality management plan for the MX1 Portable X-Ray System (“MX1”), Project P01. The Project Quality Plan documents the necessary information required to effectively manage project quality.
Scope
This document describes the specific quality steps and actions required within the MX1 project. The MX1 project is outlined in Project Plan PLN-P01-060.

Background
All activities, processes, and procedures involved in the project will be planned to fulfill the requirements of the FDA Quality System Regulations (21 CFR 820) as well as international regulations including but not limited to those for Canada (Canada Medical Devices Regulations (SOR/98-282)) and Australia (Therapeutic Goods (Medical Devices) Regulations 2002), as MedAI’s Quality Management System is certified to ISO 13485:2016 for the requirements listed in the U.S., Canadian, and Australian regulations.
Since the MX1 Portable X-Ray System is both an electronic product and a medical device, MedAI must comply with medical device requirements and country-specific radiation safety requirements.
The procedures (QSPs) to be used are defined in the MedAI Quality Manual.
Intended Use, Indications for Use and Contraindications can be found in the MX1 Regulatory Plan, PLN-P01-061.
Device Classification, Regulation Number and applicable Product Codes are covered in the MX1 Regulatory Plan, PLN-P01-061.
Project Quality Management Plan
DHF Audits
DHF audits will be conducted by Quality within five business days of the close of every development phase in accordance with QSP-018 Design Controls, and QSF-066 Design History File Checklist template. All deliverables within the subject phase must be released into MedAI’ controlled document system prior to QA approval of the DHF phase audit.
The project-specific DHF checklist is DHF-P01-008.
Manufacturing
Primary Manufacturing Obligations
MedAI will be the legal manufacturer of the MX1 Portable X-Ray System. To meet the business and sales requirements MedAI has selected Sanmina Corporation as the Contract Manufacturer (CM) of the MX1 device.
Sanmina met all required and preferred qualifications listed in the Project Plan, PLN-P01-060.
Responsibilities of the Contract Manufacturer (CM) as the Assembler
The responsibilities of the CM shall be agreed upon and outlined in a contractual quality agreement between the CM and MedAI.
Design Transfer Plan
MedAI shall engage the CM to initiate Supplier Contract Requirements/SOW, as well as a Quality Plan to define which party is responsible for what aspects of the overall product Quality during production.
Draft product work instructions, inspection criteria and equipment, in-process and finished device test procedures, and a pFMEA shall be completed before design Phase 4 closure. The documents shall be reviewed by the CM to determine if any further changes need be made based on their internal design for manufacturability assessment.
Final documentation, equipment, inventory, etc. shall be transferred to the CM during Phase 4. Production for human use may be initiated after completion of Phase 4 deliverables; however, builds may begin “at-risk” prior to regulatory clearance/approvals if desired by the business; these builds must be held under quarantine until regulatory clearance/approval is obtained.
Inspection Requirements
Inspection levels for components will be selected in accordance with QSP-014 - Inspection and Testing and QSP-026 - Statistical Techniques. Inspection points as well as sampling levels will be indicated in QSR-026 - Lot Acceptance Sampling. For any complex inspection/test methods, separate work instructions will be generated and referenced in QSR-026. For off the shelf components, component inspection specifications may reference a general receiving work instruction with limited requirements such as verification of part number, quantity, etc.
Reporting Requirements
Assembly reporting
MedAI is not required to submit reports of assembly (FDA Form 2579) for the MX1 device to FDA.
Per FDA Guidance Assembler’s Guide to Diagnostic X-Ray Equipment 2011 - Question 39, if the manufacturer has stated in its product report (FDA Form 3626) submitted to the FDA and in its assembler/user information that no assembly or installation is required, then no report of assembly is required.
MedAI is responsible for certifying these systems to meet applicable regulations and reporting the product to FDA.
MedAI shall follow state reporting requirements where applicable. Individual reporting requirements related to the sale of radiographic equipment is documented in QSP-028 - X-ray Equipment: Manufacturing Reporting Requirements.
For X-Ray Systems, systems records and reports are required if a manufacturer has the option and certifies the system as permitted in 21 CFR 1020.30 (exercises c).
FDA Form 3626 and FDA Annual Reporting
After 510(k) clearance and prior to introduction of MX1 into US commerce, MedAI shall submit an initial product report via FDA Form 3626 (21 CFR 1002.10). There is no approval process for this report. The form must simply be submitted to begin commerce (per pre-submission Q181796 FDA written feedback). This process, as well as FDA annual reporting requirements for X-ray equipment sales, is outlined in QSP-028 - X-ray Equipment: Manufacturing Reporting Requirements.
Servicing and Installation
The MX1 device shall not be serviceable by users/operators. Units that require servicing shall be returned to MedAI per QSP-013 - Servicing to determine the amount and type of repair/rework required to place the device back in service for the remaining shelf-life. Alternatively, the unit may be serviced in-field or remotely per QSP-013 by MedAI personnel.
The MX1 device will not require installation. MX1 will arrive at the customer fully assembled and ready to operate. The MX1 Instructions for Use will detail any set-up procedures or inspections required prior to use.
Postmarket Surveillance
The MX1 Portable X-Ray System shall have postmarket surveillance performed according to WI-004 - Post Market Surveillance. Additional surveillance activities to be performed in accordance with QSP-018, Design Controls, are:
Postmarket Surveillance (PMS) Plan.
A PMS Report will be generated annually as applicable after the product has been released for commercial distribution.
Postmarket Surveillance shall be both reactive to include customer complaints, enhancement requests, quality metric trends and vigilance reporting, as well as proactive to include feedback from customers and review of annual adverse event reporting of like devices.
Postmarket Surveillance shall be evaluated annually throughout the product life and may be reviewed during Management Reviews.
Additional Quality Planning
Risk Management Planning is documented in the PLN-P01-063 - Risk Management Plan.
A Summary of Known Use problems is documented as MEMO-P00-633 Rev. A.
Test Fixture Validation
Any custom test fixture produced by MedAI for use during inspection, in-process or final assembly testing shall be validated for its intended use prior to product distribution. Records of the fixture validation shall be stored within the Quality System Database. A review of equipment and test fixture validation requirements will be documented in a MX1 Master Validation plan, which is required in design and development Phase 3 per QSP-018 Rev. G, Design Control.
The CM shall either build their own test fixtures based on fixture drawings and work instructions provided by MedAI or be provided with the MedAI custom test fixtures.
Process Validation
Where responsibility for process validation is outsourced to a contract manufacturer, then the procedures and forms in use by that contract manufacturer may be used on behalf of MedAI.
Components received where the Critical to Quality requirements of the supplied component cannot be fully verified shall then require that component supplier to furnish the process validation for the process used, including but not limited to machining,  injection molding or 3D printing.
A review of processes that require validation will be documented in the MX1 Master Validation plan, which shall be generated in design Phase 3 per QSP-018 Rev. G.
MX1 Product Quality Objectives
MX1 Product Quality Objectives shall be defined in the Postmarket Surveillance Plan.
