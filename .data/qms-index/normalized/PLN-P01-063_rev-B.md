Purpose
This document is intended to provide the Risk Management Plan for the MX1 Portable X-Ray System, Project P01. The Risk Management Plan documents the necessary information required to effectively manage project risk.
This Risk Management Plan defines how risks associated with the MX1 Portable X-Ray System (“MX1”) will be identified, analyzed, and managed. It outlines how risk management activities will be performed, recorded, and monitored throughout the lifecycle of the project and provides templates and practices for recording and prioritizing risks.
Scope
This document describes the specific risk management steps and action needed to identify, control and assess risks associated with Project P01.

Definitions
Risk Management: Systematic application of management policies, procedures, and practices to the tasks of analyzing, evaluating, controlling and monitoring risk.
Occurrence: the probability of occurrence for the identified failure mode/hazard.
Severity: the effect/harm that the failure mode/hazard may have on the patient, the user/lay operator, property, or environment
Risk Management Team (RMT): team performing risk management that is typically made up of representatives with the knowledge and experience of the particular medical device, its use, and the technologies involved and/or risk management techniques. The team consists of representatives of several functions to include representatives of each engineering function, QA/RA, product management, labeling, each contributing their specialist knowledge.

Planning for Risk Assessment with the design
The risk management activities shall coincide with the MedAI Design Control procedure per QSP-018 - Design Controls. The risk management deliverables demonstrated in the table below shall be reviewed during their corresponding phase review.
The Risk Management Team (RMT) will ensure that risks are actively identified, analyzed, and managed throughout the life of the project.  Risks will be identified as early as possible in the project so as to minimize their impact.  The steps for accomplishing this are outlined in the following sections.
The MX1 RMT shall conduct the Risk Assessment for each new product or product family following QSP-019 - Risk Management Procedure. The analysis shall be summarized per QSF-017 - Risk Management Report.

Risk Evaluation
Once Severity and Occurrence rankings have been assigned, the Risk of each failure mode/hazard shall be evaluated using the Risk Evaluation Ranking Guide in Table 4 below. The resulting risk number shall be evaluated against the Risk Acceptability and Risk Control (see Table 5 below) to determine the required course of action. All risks, regardless of the initial risk rating, shall be reduced as far as possible. The reduction of each individual risk shall be considered, together with all other risks, against the benefit of the device.
In addition to determining the Risk for each failure mode/hazard, a Risk Priority Number (RPN) shall be assigned for each failure mode/hazard by multiplying the Severity and Occurrence rankings. The RPN shall be used for prioritization in addressing the risk control of failure modes/hazards. In addition to the magnitude of the RPN, the decision for mitigation is primarily influenced by the severity of the failure mode.

Risk Control/Reduction
For all risks one or more of the following risk control measures shall be used in the priority order listed:
Inherent Safety by Design
Protective Measures in the medical device itself or in the manufacturing process
Information for safety (The least preferred protective measure is a written warning or contraindication. Information for safety is generally not considered an effective measure for reducing the occurrence of risks, but can be referenced in the file when applicable.)
NOTE: If information for safety is the only risk control measure used, then none of the S or O rankings may change in the residual risk assessment to account for a user’s “free will”.
Risk controls shall be tested through Verification/Validation.
Implementation/Effectiveness
The MX1 RMT shall manage and verify that all risk control measures are properly implemented. This may be done by adding the controlled document number that provides evidence of the implemented risk control measures/mitigation section of the Risk Assessment. These documents may include, but are not limited to released drawings that demonstrate the design change or testing (internal or 3rd party) that verifies the risk control.
Residual Risk Evaluation
Any residual risk that remains after risk control measures are applied shall be evaluated using the Risk Evaluation Ranking Guide (Table 4 above) to determine if the risk has been reduced to an acceptable level - moderate or low priority (whichever is considered “as far as possible”). Any individual risks that cannot be reduced to an acceptable level must be addressed in the Risk-Benefit Analysis.
Risk Evaluation
A Risk/Benefit analysis shall be initiated if the residual risk cannot be reduced to an acceptable level. The risk will remain unacceptable If this evidence does not support the conclusion that the medical benefits outweigh the risk. Any further acceptable residual risks must be disclosed to the users/patients of the device via the Accompanying Documentation, Instructions for Use and/or product labeling. The Risk/Benefit Evaluation may be documented separately, or as a part of the Risk Management Report and filed within the Design History File for the MX1 device.
Risk Management Report
Once the Risk Assessment is complete, then QA/RA shall document the results in a Risk Management Report in accordance with QSF-017-Risk Management Report Template and provide an overall conclusion of the acceptability of risk for the product. The pFMEA and dFMEA will be documented separately within the design history file or as separate tabs within the MX1 hazard analysis document per QSF-018. Once complete, both the Risk Assessment and Risk Management Report shall be filed within the Design History File for Project P01.
Production and Post Market Surveillance
The level of risk for the MX1 Portable X-Ray System shall be tracked, monitored and reported throughout the project lifecycle in accordance with WI-004-Post Market Surveillance.  All Engineering Change Requests shall be analyzed for their possible impact to the product’s risks. Management will be notified of important changes to risk status as a component to Management Reviews.
Additional postmarket feedback to include complaint data, quality trends and adverse event reporting for both MX1 and other, like devices shall be evaluated to determine if previously unrecognized hazards are present, if the estimated risk(s) arising from a hazard are no longer acceptable, or if the original assessment has changed (modification of S or O levels based on post market data).
Cybersecurity Risk Management
Traditional risk management approaches utilizing ISO 14971 would not be appropriate to evaluate software cybersecurity due to the deliberate nature of the attacker. To manage these risks, a Cybersecurity Risk Analysis/Threat Model shall be performed to evaluate the various attack surfaces the MX1 Portable X-Ray system may expose, as well as the software design features that mitigate these potential threats. All activities within risk management shall still be utilized from risk assessment and evaluation to risk control and residual risk evaluation, with the main difference being that traditional risk management approaches consider risks in the design, user workflow and production processes, while cybersecurity risk management shall consider risks due to vulnerabilities. This analysis shall follow the procedures of conducting risk using QSP-019 - Risk Management and WI-001-Risk Analysis and Evaluation, while utilizing the specific security risk assessment methods described in AAMI TIR 57 and consider additional cybersecurity risk management guidance from ANSI/CAN/UL 2900-1 and -2-1.
Once the Cybersecurity Risk Analysis/Threat Model is complete, Software Engineering shall produce a Cybersecurity Risk Management Report to summarize the cybersecurity risk assessment, evaluation and mitigation activities that assure the MX1 System is reasonably secure and therefore, is a trustworthy device.
Usability Risk Management
Risks related to user error shall be evaluated per ISO 14971 following IEC 62366-1:2015. Analysis and verification of the risks and risk controls shall be performed in accordance with PLN-P01-064 - MX1 Usability Plan. Use-related risk analysis will be documented in accordance with QSF-017 - Risk Management Report and QSF-018 Risk and Hazard Analysis Template. Identification of known use errors will be documented separately in a MEMO.
Accessory Risk Management
Over the course of the MX1 design life cycle accessories may be added. The risk of the addition of any new accessory to the device shall be documented in accordance with QSF-017 - Risk Management Report and QSF-018 - Risk and Hazard Analysis Template and submitted by engineering change request (ECR) via WI-005 - Engineering Change Request. The accessories shall be evaluated for risks per IEC 60601-1 requirements and QSP-019 - Risk Management. Accessory risk analysis will be documented separately or as separate tabs within the MX1 device hazard analysis per QSF-018, however interoperability risks must be considered and documented within the parent device hazard analysis in accordance with Guidance for Industry and FDA Staff - Design Considerations and Premarket Submission Recommendations for Interoperable Medical Devices (2017).
