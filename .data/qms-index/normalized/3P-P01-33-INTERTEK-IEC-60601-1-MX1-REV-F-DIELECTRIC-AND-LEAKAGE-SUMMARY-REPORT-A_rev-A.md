Purpose
The purpose of this assessment was to evaluate the dielectric and leakage performance of the MX1 Portable X-ray System to ensure a modification to a power isolation component in the C1 Cassette did not affect electrical safety.
Scope and Background
This assessment applies to the MX1 Rev F Portable X-Ray System, which consists of the E1 Emitter, C1 Cassette, H1 Wired Charger (Qty 2) and Collimation Pucks. The F1 Foot Pedal and K1 Cart were provided as accessories to the MX1. T1 Tablet was provided as support equipment.
Performance and reliability improvements incorporated into MX1 Rev F were risk assessed in PLN-P01-065 - MX1 Verification & Validation Plan. One Cassette Main PCBA improvement, the isolated DC/DC converter used for generating a 5V power rail on the isolated USB port region of the design, triggered repeating 60601-1 Dielectric and Leakage verification testing.
This assessment was performed in accordance with with the following standard:
Medical Electrical Equipment - Part 1: General Requirements for Basic Safety and Essential Performance [IEC 60601-1:2005 Ed.3+A2:2020]
Essential Performance testing is not required before and after dielectric and leakage testing per the IEC 60601-1.

Procedure and Results Summary
The following MX1 Portable X-Ray System components and supporting equipment were provided to Intertek to be used for the assessment.
This assessment was conducted by Intertek Testing Services (Intertek Testing Services NA, Inc, 300 Pine Boulevard, Suite 100, Rivertown, IL 60003 USA).
Device configuration for all testing was the final finished version.  All electrical safety testing included a Collimation Puck.
Device was built with all WorkStations with the exception of the End of Line WorkStation as camera, detector, and focal spot calibrations, and laser alignment were not required.
MedAI Equipment and Materials
The MX1 Portable X-ray System Rev F  passed all Dielectric and Leakage safety evaluations per  IEC 60601-1.
Based on the Intertek Safety Test Report in Attachments 1, the MX1 Portable X-ray System Rev F complies with the requirements indicated in IEC 60601-1:2005 Ed.3+A1+A2.
