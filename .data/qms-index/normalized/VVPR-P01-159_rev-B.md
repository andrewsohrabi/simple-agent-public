Protocol Section
OBJECTIVE
To ensure that the MX1 System meets all weight limits established in DR-P01-005 - Design Inputs & Specifications.
REFERENCES
DR-P01-005 Rev. A - MX1 Design Inputs
QSP-026 Rev. B - Statistical Techniques
MATERIALS
Materials & Equipment

MX1 Cassette (C1) Rev. H
MX1 Emitter (E1) Rev. I
MX1 Foot Pedal (F1) Rev. G
MX1 Pucks (MS-10627) Rev. C
MX1 Case (P1) Rev. E
MX1 Wired Charger (H1) Rev. C Qty 2
Heavy Duty Scale - EQP-101 or equivalent
SAMPLE SIZE
For this test, one (1) MX1 Portable X-ray System will be used as all devices contain the same components and the weight of one device adequately represents the device weight.
METHODS
Locations and Personnel Responsibilities
This study shall take place in MedAI, Inc. facilities in Springfield, IL.
Engineering is responsible for execution of the protocol.
Training Requirements
This study does not require any special training.
Experimental Procedure
Record all equipment and materials used in data collection form in Appendix A.
Using EQP-101 or equivalent, weigh the following components and mark them in Table 1:
Emitter (E1)
Cassette (C1)
Case (P1)
Fully packaged MX1 System (E1, all pucks, C1, P1, H1 (qty 2)) (MX1)
Foot pedal (F1).
